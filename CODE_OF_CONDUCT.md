# Code of Conduct

## Our values

The development of ProfoundData and all associated activities — including discussions on the issue tracker, pull request reviews, and interactions in any other forum related to this project — is grounded in the following values:

**Scientific integrity.** ProfoundData provides access to a database used in forest and ecological modelling research. We are committed to maintaining correctness, transparency, and reproducibility in the package, the database and their documentation. Issues, corrections, and improvements are always welcome.

**Free and open knowledge.** ProfoundData is free software and is developed in the spirit of open science. We believe that scientific tools and data should be openly accessible and that knowledge should be shared freely.

**Mutual respect.** All participants in this project are expected to engage respectfully and in good faith, regardless of background, level of expertise, or point of view. Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, dismissive language, and bad-faith engagement are not.

## Human authorship

Interactions in this repository — including issues, comments, and pull requests — are expected to come from people who take personal responsibility for their content. Submissions from outside the ProfoundData team that were generated autonomously by AI agents or automated tools are not appropriate and will be closed or deleted without review. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details on the policy regarding AI-generated contributions.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all spaces associated with ProfoundData, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channels used for the project.

## Reporting

If you observe behavior that violates these values, please contact the maintainer directly by e-mail (run `maintainer("ProfoundData")` in R to get the address). Reports will be handled with discretion.
